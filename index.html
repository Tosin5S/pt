STONE AGE FIELD GUIDE

Mastering Your Prehistoric Tribe

1. Selecting & Commanding 👋

Click any Villager (or use the bottom roster bar) to select them. Then click any Tree, Boulder, Berry Bush, Animal, Building, or Raider to assign them! Drag the map or use WASD / Arrow Keys to pan.

2. Kitchen & Hunger 🍖

Foraged berries and hunted meat give Raw Food (🍓). Assign a villager to the Kitchen to roast 1 Raw Food into 2 Sabre-Tooth Ribs (🍖)! Hungry villagers automatically eat Cooked Ribs to restore Stamina & Health.

3. Huts & Growing the Tribe 👶

Each Hut adds +3 Max Population. Assign tired villagers to a Hut to sleep, or click "❤️ Grow Tribe" on a Hut (or send 2 adults inside) to spawn a Cave Child who grows into a full worker!

4. Prehistoric Gym & Armoury 💪

Build a Stone Gym so villagers can lift stone barbells and permanently level up Strength (STR) and Speed! Build an Armoury to craft Wooden Clubs and Bone Spears.

5. Cannibal Raids & Net Traps 🕸️

Watch the countdown timer! Rival cannibals attack periodically. Place Net Traps around your perimeter to snare raiders, and ring the 📯 War Horn to rally all warriors to defend!

6. 2008 Review Easter Egg 🦋

As noted in the Pocket Gamer review of Prehistoric Tribes, cavemen in 2008 sometimes wandered away from half-built huts! Toggle between Smart AI and 2008 Wander Mode anytime on the top bar.
