STONE AGE FIELD GUIDE

Mobile Touch Controls & Articulated Tribe Survival

1. Touch & Macro Camera 👋

1-Finger Tap selects a human or assigns them to chop, mine, hunt, build, or fight. 1-Finger Drag pans across the 4800×3200 valley with smooth inertia. 2-Finger Pinch zooms between `0.30x` macro view and `1.65x` closeup!

2. Hauling, Kitchen & Ribs 🍖

Watch your humans visibly carry logs, stones, and meat in both hands! Assign a villager to the Kitchen to roast Raw Food into 2 Sabre-Tooth Ribs (🍖).

3. Huts & Growing the Tribe 👶

Each Fur Hut adds +3 Max Population. Tap "❤️ Grow Tribe" on a selected Hut to pair two adults and welcome a Cave Child who grows into an adult worker!

4. Stone Gym & Armoury 💪

At the Stone Gym, humans press heavy stone barbells overhead with both hands to gain permanent STR & SPD levels! Craft Clubs and Bone Spears at the Armoury.
